Car painting in Tallinn since [2012]

Mpaint is a car painting and body repair workshop in Tallinn. We specialise in painting a whole car or individual parts, and we carry out every painting job in our own paint booth so the finish stays even and free of dust.

Most people come to us after a parking scrape, an accident, or when rust has started to show along an edge or wheel arch. We match your factory colour code, prepare the panel properly, and return the car looking the way it did before the damage. We also polish faded paint and cloudy headlights, pull dents with a spotter without repainting where the paint is still intact, and replace body parts that are past repair.

We are a repair partner for Seesam, ERGO, If and Swedbank, so if your repair goes through insurance we can handle the claim paperwork directly. Send us a photo of the damage and we will come back with a price and a timeline, usually within 24 hours on working days.

How car painting works at Mpaint

Every repaint starts with an assessment of the damage and a read of your car's factory colour code. We prepare and sand the panel, apply primer, then spray base coat and lacquer inside a dedicated paint booth where filtered air and controlled temperature keep dust out of the finish.

We paint single panels as well as full cars. Painting one door, bumper or wing is usually enough after a parking scrape, while corrosion or accident damage across several panels is better handled as a larger job. If the paint on your car has faded unevenly, we blend into the neighbouring panels so the repair does not stand out.

Alongside painting we handle welding and sheet metal repair, rust removal and anti-corrosion treatment, spotter dent pulling, body and headlight polishing, and replacement of body parts. We work with Seesam, ERGO, If and Swedbank on insurance claims and deal with the paperwork on your behalf.

What a finished repair should look like

A repair is done properly when you cannot tell where it starts. The colour matches under daylight as well as under street lighting, the texture of the lacquer matches the panels next to it, and the gaps between panels stay even. That is the standard we work to on every car that leaves the workshop.

Most of the jobs shown here are everyday repairs rather than show cars: parking scrapes, rear quarter damage after a low speed collision, rust along a wheel arch, faded bumpers and cloudy headlights. If you want to see a job similar to yours before deciding, call us and we will show you examples from the workshop.

Common questions

How much does it cost to paint a car?

The price depends on how many panels are involved, the condition of the surface underneath and the type of paint. A single panel is far cheaper than a full respray. Metallic and pearl finishes take more material and more time than a solid colour. Send a photo of the damage and we will quote the actual job rather than a guess.

How long does the car stay at the workshop?

A single panel is usually a couple of days including preparation, painting and curing. Larger jobs involving bodywork or several panels take longer. We give you a timeline together with the price so you can plan around it.

Will the new paint match the rest of the car?

We work from your factory colour code and blend into the surrounding panels where needed. On older cars the original paint has usually faded, so blending is what makes the repair invisible rather than matching the code alone.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes. We are a repair partner for Seesam, ERGO, If and Swedbank, and we handle the claim paperwork for you. Bring the case number and we will take it from there.

Can you remove a dent without repainting?

Often yes. If the paint surface is not broken we can pull the dent with a spotter and leave the original paint intact, which is faster and cheaper than refinishing the panel.

Getting to the workshop

Our workshop is in Tallinn and we serve drivers from across Harju county. You are welcome to come by during opening hours to have damage looked at in person, though sending a photo first usually saves you a trip: we can normally give a price and a timeline before you drive over.

We speak Estonian, Russian and English. Phone and WhatsApp are the quickest way to reach us during the working day, and email is best if you want to attach several photos or an insurance case number. If you call outside opening hours, leave a message and we will call back the next working morning.
